# Conference Pain Points

## Registration Module

### Features

#### Initial Information Collection

- **Mobile Number Collection:** Collect the mobile number of the attendee initially.

#### Integration

- **WhatsApp Alerts:** Incorporate WhatsApp for sending timely alerts and updates to attendees.

### Basic Details Collection

#### Paid Facilities

- **Banquet:** Option to register for the banquet.
- **Pre-Conference Activities:** Option to register for various activities conducted before the conference.
- Some conferences might have the option to book any number of facilities separately (for example: I can book multiple banquets for my friends, and my friends need not be a registered attendee/delegate)

#### Workshop Management

- **Count Limit:** Limit the number of participants in each workshop.
- **Registration Flexibility:** Allow attendees to register for workshops at any time, including those who registered for the event earlier.
- **Conditional Registration:**
    - Only one workshop at a time can be selected.
    - Attendees can choose both morning and evening sessions.
    - Workshops limited to certain categories of attendees.

#### Hotel Management

- **Accommodation Options:**
  - **Single:** Single room accommodation.
  - **Double Sharing:** Room shared with another attendee.
  - **Double Without Sharing:** Double room for single use, available in case of accommodation requirements.
- **Count:** Track the number of rooms available and booked.

### Connections Between Facilities, Workshops and Hotel Booking

- Dependecy between these items. Examples:
  - One facility can be choosen only if another facility is choosen.
  - One facility cannot be choosen if another one selected.
  - Some workshops might require some facility also
  - Some facility might require hotel booking 
  - etc

#### Tax Management

- **GST Inclusive:** Option to show prices inclusive of GST.
- **GST Exclusive:** Option to show prices exclusive of GST.
- **No GST:** Option to show prices without applying GST.

### Category Management

- Ability to manage different categories of attendees and assign specific permissions or restrictions to each category.

### Slab Creation

- Define different pricing slabs or tiers for registration fees, based on factors such as early bird, regular,or late registration.

### Reporting

- Generate detailed reports on registrations, payments, workshop enrollments, accommodation bookings, and other relevant data.
